sort1 uses: Bubble Sort

How do you know?: Performs way better on ordered than reversed and random. By design, bubble finishes quickly when the list is already sorted.

sort2 uses: Merge Sort

How do you know?: Performs very well on the *.50000 files compares to the 2 others. No specifc differences between sorted, reversed and random (the process is the same in any case)

sort3 uses: Selection Sort

How do you know?: Performs equally in reversed, ordered and random. Way more difficult when the size of the file is bigger (compares to merge sort).
